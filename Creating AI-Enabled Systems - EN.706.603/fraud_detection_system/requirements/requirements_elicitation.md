### Task 1: Elicitation and Gathering Requirements 

#### Task 1.1: Identify the TWO stakeholders for the fraud detection system. Describe why you selected these stakeholders, and describe how you would engage with these stakeholders to gather requirements.

#### Task 1.2: List the types of questions you would ask the stakeholders to uncover both functional and non-functional requirements for the fraud detection system. List TWO questions for each identified stakeholder. 

---

* #### Stakeholder 1
  #### Customer Service Department

  Reason: The customer service department, who regularly interacts with the customers of the Bank, would be able to more accurately identify the needs and complaints of the user without contacting the user directly in the initial stages of developing the project.

  Engagement: Depending on the size of the customer service department. If the department is quite large, I would begin with a survey, followed by interviews and feedback sessions with key members of the department (i.e. managers, supervisors, leading customer service representatives)

  * Question 1: How do you handle situations where customers dispute legitimate transactions believing them to be fraudulent, and how often does it happen?
    
  * Question 2: What specific information do you need in a fraud alert to quickly and accurately assist a customer?

* #### Stakeholder 2:
  #### Fraud Detection Team (Developers of Current Model)

  Reason: The Fraud Detection Team encounters the pitfalls of the current model on a daily basis. Their work is directly impacted by the performance of the model. They would also be able to give the most insight on the current model and its system set-up.

  Engagement: Interviews and brainstorming sessions may work best with the fraud detection team. Brainstorming sessions have the potential to iron out previously made mistakes with the current model and allow for detailed illustrations of the challenges of the department.

  * Question 1: How often was the current model retrained with updated, recent information?

  * Question 2: How do you assess the risk of new types of fraud that may not be covered by existing models?
